# Product brief

**AFTERGLOW** — _Every memory leaves a little light behind._ /
_Cada recuerdo deja un poquito de luz._

## What it is

A reusable storytelling engine that turns a creator's memories, photographs, messages
and (optional) music into a small interactive world for one person. The first
experience is a birthday dedication for Isa, a best friend.

It is not a template with names swapped in: the occasion, relationship, language,
scenes, order, palette and content are all configuration. The engine assumes no
birthday, gender, relationship type, language, photo count or scene count.

## Who it is for

- **Recipient** — opens a link, often on a phone, maybe with sound off. Must understand
  how to move forward immediately and never wait on animation to read something.
- **Creator** — comfortable editing a file and dropping photos into a folder; not
  necessarily a developer. Publishes for free on GitHub Pages under a URL of their
  choosing (e.g. `wembie.github.io/mejoramigaisa`).

## Journey (default)

Arrival → invitation (+ optional music) → memories → shared history (timeline) →
the Tree of Memories → names and inside jokes → our constellation → a letter →
birthday finale → closing. Any subset, any order.

## Principles

1. **Content first.** Photos and words are the point; effects support them.
2. **Earned emotion.** The finale gathers the lights collected on the way; the closing
   brings back each memory that was opened.
3. **Never trapped.** Skip, back, chapters, close and replay are always reachable.
4. **Optional everything.** Sound, motion and WebGL-class effects are progressive.
5. **Honest privacy.** A public link is public; the tool says so.

## Scope of v1.0.0 (implemented)

Nine scene types, validated configuration, per-scene themes, particle engine, audio
manager with generative music, memory dialog/gallery, procedural tree and constellation,
editorial timeline, kinetic typography, preferences (motion, quality, volumes, reading
mode), per-deployment URLs and publishing, tests, documentation.

## Out of scope

Backends, uploads, analytics, accounts, password protection, CMS, video backgrounds.

## Content status for Isa

All personal content (memories, dates, names, letter, photos) is **placeholder** and
marked `placeholder: true`. Nothing about Isa has been invented. The creator fills it in.
